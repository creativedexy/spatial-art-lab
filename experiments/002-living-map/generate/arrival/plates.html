Phase 13 · measured 22 September 2026

In plain English

Phase 12 was signed off from 340 to 450 m up. Putting the camera on the ground is the first honest test of that model, and it failed in two places and passed in one that the written design had ruled out.

The design said The Homes had no destination, because when it was written there were 70 repeated house footprints. There are now 1,030 dwellings on internal streets, so there is a street to stand in, and it is the best arrival in the set.

The two arrivals the design did choose are dead where they stand: mature orchard trees are planted exactly where both cameras are, 5 and 9 m away, so the frame is leaves. That is the model being right and the camera being wrong.

The panel field is the real finding. From the air those rows read as a fine dark stripe. At head height they are 2 m opaque black walls with mown grass between them, which is not what vertical agrivoltaics are: the crop between the rows is the whole argument. That is being fixed now.
