Use the built-in image_gen tool, sketch-to-render, to make a photograph of a place in
2045 that no camera has ever stood in, from a render of our own measured 3D model.

INPUT 1 is the geometry master: a render of our 3D model from the exact camera this
photograph is taken at, eye height 1.6 m above the measured terrain, 48 degree vertical
field of view. Follow the plate for WHAT IS WHERE, within about 20 px at 1280x720.
INPUT 2 is the approved adventurous target board for the panels and glasshouses:
materials and character only, not layout.

THIS PLACE: standing between the rows of a vertical agrivoltaic field on the edge of the
new neighbourhood, 2045. Measured: 9.7 hectares, 39 rows in 3 fields, rows 11 m apart on
a 22 degree bearing, modules 2.0 m tall standing 0.35 m clear of the ground, 1.1 m wide
in a frame, on posts at 3.6 m centres. The new homes stand 86 to 155 m behind, including
a 20.4 m apartment block; their roofs carry solar glass.

What the photograph must show, because the model says it is there:
- Bifacial modules standing vertically, edge on to the camera, reading as blue-black
  glass that mirrors the sky at a glancing angle rather than as black fence panels. The
  gap under each row is real and you can see the crop through it.
- The 11 m between rows FARMED, which is the entire point of vertical agrivoltaics: a
  worked cereal or legume crop in drilled rows running along the field, with a narrow
  uncultivated service band either side of each panel line. Next2Sun at Donaueschingen is
  the precedent. Not mown grass, not gravel, not bare soil.
- Slim galvanised posts and rails, no concrete ballast blocks, no security fence.
- The homes behind at the right size for their distance, roofs with solar glass, trees
  between.
- One or two people at true scale: a grower walking the crop rows, someone on a bike on
  the field track. Ordinary work, not a photoshoot.

Design direction: design excellence and realism, built precedents only (Next2Sun,
Solarsiedlung, Goldsmith Street for the homes behind). Never a solar-farm stock photo,
never an illustration, never the developer's marketing style. No text or watermark. Late
summer afternoon at about 16:45, long shadows across the crop, hazy sky, no HDR glow.

Forbid: tilted panels on racks (these are vertical); a continuous black wall; panels
taller than 2 m or closer together than 11 m; bare gravel or membrane between the rows;
sheep instead of crop; a security fence; sky more than 30 per cent of frame; any building
moved, added or removed from the plate.

Output 1280x720 (whole-frame resize only, no crop or warp) to
experiments/002-living-map/generate/arrival/out/among-the-panels.png. One generation plus
at most one correction pass if geometry drifts. Write
experiments/002-living-map/generate/arrival/out/among-the-panels-notes.md with the prompt
verbatim, the passes, and an honest inspection.
