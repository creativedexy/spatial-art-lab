Use the built-in image_gen tool, sketch-to-render, to make a photograph of a place in
2045 that no camera has ever stood in, from a render of our own measured 3D model.

INPUT 1 is the geometry master: a render of our 3D model from the exact camera this
photograph is taken at, eye height 1.6 m above the measured terrain, 48 degree vertical
field of view. Every building edge, ground line and tree position must keep its place
within about 20 px at 1280x720. Follow the plate for WHAT IS WHERE.
INPUT 2 is the approved adventurous target board for the campus: materials and character
only, not layout.

THIS PLACE: the court between two buildings of the innovation campus, 2045. Measured:
105,501 m2 of campus floor in blocks of 2 to 7 storeys; the two in this frame are 17.0 m
and 13.6 m, 40 and 60 m from the camera; their east elevations, so at this hour they are
in their own shade with the sun raking across the upper floors.

What the photograph must show:
- Silvered larch and buff brick over a glazed ground floor, deep reveals, a floor-depth
  spandrel; never curtain wall, never an office park.
- The court itself doing work: sett and gravel paths through long wildflower grass,
  retained mature trees, timber seating at the glazed ground floor, cycle stands, a
  covered way. Not a lawn and not a plaza of paving.
- The shade read as shade: cool blue-grey in the court, warm light catching the parapets
  and the trees behind. This is the real light at 16:45 in late August, not a fault.
- People at true scale: campus workers crossing, two sitting outside the ground floor,
  someone wheeling a bike. Ordinary, few, unposed.

Design direction: design excellence and realism, built precedents only (Sara Kulturhus,
Goldsmith Street, Powerhouse Brattorkaia). Never a corporate render, never an
illustration, never the developer's marketing style. No text or watermark. No HDR glow.

Forbid: glass box towers; a car park; a hard empty plaza; lens flare; people as specks;
a building taller, shorter or moved from the plate; sky more than 25 per cent of frame.

Output 1280x720 (whole-frame resize only, no crop or warp) to
experiments/002-living-map/generate/arrival/out/the-court.png. One generation plus at most
one correction pass if geometry drifts. Write
experiments/002-living-map/generate/arrival/out/the-court-notes.md with the prompt
verbatim, the passes, and an honest inspection.
