Arrival

Three places you can stand in, 1.6 m above the measured ground, 48 degree lens. Each photograph was generated from the plate beside it, and the map flies to that same camera.

Phase 13 · 22 September 2026

In plain English

Everything in this map so far was seen from 340 m up or more. These are the first three pictures of it from head height, and they are where generation finally earns its place: no camera can photograph 2045, and no aerial shot can show you a street.

The model had to be changed to deserve them. At head height the solar rows were 2 m black walls over mown grass, which is the opposite of what vertical agrivoltaics are for. They now reflect the sky as light rather than as paint, carry their module rhythm and posts, and the land between the rows is farmed.

The street is the one the written design said did not exist. It was right when it was written and wrong by the time it was read, because phase 12 built 1,030 homes on real streets in between.

The three arrivals

The street From the-homes

38 buildings in frame. Facing terraces 23 m apart, the nearest 20 m away rising 25 degrees, an open stormwater channel 8 m off in the verge. 1,030 dwellings, terraces of 2.5 and 3 storeys with 5 and 7 storey blocks among them.

The court From the-campus

The 17.0 m and 13.6 m campus blocks, 40 and 60 m away, in the one clearing of the campus orchard. 105,501 m2 of campus floor, every block placed by one rule: within reach of GCHQ and a road.

Among the panels From the-panels

Vertical bifacial modules 2.0 m tall, 0.35 m clear of the ground, 11 m apart on the fields' own 22 degree grain, and the 11 m between them still farmed. 9.7 hectares, 39 rows, the new homes 86 to 155 m behind.

What had to change to make the third one honest

The first attempt mixed a sky colour into the panel's albedo behind a gate that kept it away from the signed-off aerial. It changed nothing: the body is a linear 0.04 and the sun is behind the panel. Reflected sky is light, not albedo, so it is now added after the lighting stage and is not gated on where the camera is. On the held aerial that costs 2 parts in 255 of mean luminance, and nearly all of it is the field gaining the crop stripes it should always have had.
