# Kit sheet notes

## Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Asset type: architectural landscape kit sheet

Primary request: Create ONE seamless photorealistic kit sheet arranged as an exact 3 columns by 2 rows grid of six equal square close-aerial studies. No text, no labels, no captions, no logos, no borders beyond very narrow clean gutters. Each panel is a distinct built, inhabited British/European precedent-quality scene viewed obliquely from approximately 60–100 metres, like a high-resolution drone photograph.

Input images: Image 1 is the strict standard for photographic realism, crisp aerial detail, late-summer afternoon sunlight, natural shadows, atmospheric depth, meadow species and believable people/cars. Image 2 supplies only the concept of a campus landmark with a planted roof descending toward the ground; do not copy its architecture.

Overall continuity: all six panels feel photographed on the same clear late-summer afternoon, with the same realistic colour science and camera language as Image 1. Mature, ordinary landscapes; physically credible construction, drainage, planting and human scale. Built precedents, not speculative sci-fi. Quiet civic character, not corporate. Documentary architectural photography, never an illustration, diagram, collage rendering, CGI look, miniature, tilt-shift, or glossy sales image.

Panel order, left-to-right across the top row, then left-to-right across the bottom row:

1. A refined campus landmark whose broad wildflower meadow roof visibly and continuously slopes all the way down to meet the ground, fully walkable by small groups of people on discreet paths. Elegant low civic architecture integrated into terrain; improve the architectural resolution of Image 2; abundant late-summer meadow.
2. A five-storey mass-timber campus block forming a woodland courtyard. Clearly legible timber structure and deep window reveals. On the roof, photovoltaic arrays are carefully angled toward the sun with meadow planting strips between them, drawing on the construction intelligence of Sara Kulturhus and Powerhouse Brattørkaia without copying either building.
3. A coherent residential street of steep-roofed Passivhaus terraces: one roof pitch carries flush dark photovoltaic panels and the opposite pitch is slate; pale brick walls, modest planted front gardens, pavement, bicycles and mature street trees; spatial character informed by Goldsmith Street Norwich and Solarsiedlung Freiburg.
4. Parallel vertical bifacial photovoltaic panel rows over genuine grazed pasture, with sheep calmly grazing between rows; believable Next2Sun-style agrivoltaic spacing, supports, fencing, tracks, shadows and rolling field context.
5. A lived-in residential street organised around an open shallow stormwater channel and lush rain gardens that visibly flow toward a natural pond edged with reeds; bridges and paths cross the channel; credible Augustenborg Malmö-style water-sensitive landscape integrated with homes and street trees.
6. A mature native tree-palette landscape study clearly containing, in one cohesive place: one huge veteran oak with spreading crown, a formal lime avenue, a productive orchard in rows, a dense mixed native woodland clump, and a neatly managed native hedge. Paths and a few people establish scale; species and management differences are visually legible without labels.

Composition/framing: each square has its own strong close aerial composition with a visible horizon or contextual edge where suitable. Exact six-panel 3x2 layout; no merged panels, no extra panels, no dominant hero image.

Lighting/mood: warm but neutral late-summer afternoon, clear blue sky glimpses, directional sun and realistic shadows exactly in the spirit of Image 1.

Materials/textures: weathered pale brick, timber, slate, glass, meadow grasses and wildflowers, credible PV glass, native foliage, pasture and wetland plants; highly resolved real photographic texture.

Constraints: all six requested subjects must be clearly distinguishable; architecture and infrastructure must be buildable and precedent-grounded; small realistic people and occasional ordinary vehicles only where appropriate; square panels; no written characters anywhere.

Avoid: text, numbers, labels, signs, watermarks, logos, corporate campus polish, fantastical forms, excessive saturation, sterile empty spaces, cartoon vegetation, concept-art brushwork, obvious 3D-render sheen, warped buildings, duplicate people, malformed solar panels, ornamental sheep.

## Inspection

- Final export verified at 1536 × 1024 PNG; the generated master was resized only, without cropping or compositing.
- The sheet contains six equal square panels in the requested 3 × 2 order, separated by narrow white gutters, with no text or labels.
- All six subjects are legible: walkable meadow-roof landmark; five-storey timber courtyard with roof PV/meadow; pale-brick Passivhaus street; vertical agrivoltaics with sheep; residential stormwater landscape; and the mature tree palette.
- Lighting, aerial viewpoint, material detail, planting density and background context are consistent across the sheet and closely follow Input 1's late-summer photographic character.
- Minor interpretation: panel 5 reads as a generous linked channel/pond wetland rather than a narrow kerbside rill, while retaining the requested residential rain-garden drainage logic.
