# Round 9b: small animals, new environments

All six clean portrait plates were generated natively at 1024 × 1536 with the built-in image generation tool. V2, V3, and V4 from Round 7 were used only as references for the low single-source lighting, deep-black falloff, photographic macro realism, and illuminated dew or particles. Their habitats were explicitly excluded.

Each project copy was scaled to 1920 px tall and centre-cropped to 1080 × 1920 with `sips`. Final dimensions were verified for all six PNGs.

## e1 — orb-weaver in frost

- Result: An orb-weaver sits at the hub of a diagonal web stretched between dead thistle and teasel heads, with hoarfrost visible along the web threads.
- Crop check: The spider, hub, and primary web structure remain intact inside the narrow centre crop.

## e2 — huntsman on eucalyptus

- Result: A huntsman spreads flat across pale peeling eucalyptus bark, with papery bark curls and amber gum resin catching the low light.
- Crop check: The complete spider remains inside the centre crop and the diagonal trunk stays readable.

## e3 — desert scorpion

- Result: A desert scorpion crosses rippled sand with both pincers visible and its segmented tail raised against the black night.
- Crop check: Tail, body, legs, and pincers remain fully inside the centre crop.

## e4 — jumping spider on lichen

- Result: A forward-eyed jumping spider climbs a diagonal granite face among orange and grey lichen rosettes.
- Crop check: The complete animal and its eye catchlights remain safe, with the mineral-and-lichen habitat clearly distinct.

## e5 — seahorse on gorgonian

- Result: A seahorse grips a red gorgonian branch with its curled tail while the fan lattice and plankton sparks recede into black water.
- Crop check: The full seahorse, tail grip, and surrounding sea-fan structure remain visible.

## e6 — mantis on orchid

- Result: A praying mantis holds its raised raptorial forelegs above a dew-covered pale orchid on a diagonal stem.
- Crop check: The complete mantis, antennae, raised forelegs, and orchid remain within the centre crop.

## Shared direction

- One low torch-like source with rapid falloff into deep black.
- Photographic macro wildlife realism with dew, frost, sand, or suspended particles catching the light.
- Every plate uses a distinct environment.
- No ferns, moss, or forest-floor undergrowth.
- Clean plates only: no HUD, text, lines, borders, or overlays.
