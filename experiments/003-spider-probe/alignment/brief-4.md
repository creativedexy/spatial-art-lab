# Round 4: three jellyfish plates (clean, for video)

**For:** Codex, free subscription, built-in image_gen. £0. No reference images.

The piece now has two animals: spiders (plates k1 to k3 in `out3/`) and jellyfish. Each plate becomes the first frame of a 5-second video clip, then TouchDesigner and Specimen Studio track the motion and draw the HUD. So the plates carry **no HUD, no text, no lines**: only the animal and its habitat. They must sit in the same world as the spider plates: near-black, a single low light source, natural habitat in frame, photographic macro realism, gold and white highlights with the animal's own colour allowed.

Make three PNGs at 1600 × 900 in `experiments/003-spider-probe/alignment/out4/`:
- `j1-plate.png`: a moon jellyfish drifting in a dark rock pool at night, lit by one low torch from the side; kelp and a barnacled rock edge in the lower third; the bell pulsing, tentacles trailing; tiny suspended particles catching the light. Room on the right for a data twin.
- `j2-plate.png`: a compass or lion's mane jellyfish in open dark water, close and low, bioluminescent plankton sparks around it, a strand of seagrass entering from below; tentacles long and fine, left of centre.
- `j3-plate.png`: a small cluster of three comb jellies (ctenophores) with their iridescent comb rows, beside a frond of seaweed, in black water; each one clearly separate so a tracker could follow three targets.

Rules: anatomy right, no text, no frames, no lines, at least 40% of the frame near-black, the animal sharp. Write `out4/notes.md` and `out4/prompts.json`.
