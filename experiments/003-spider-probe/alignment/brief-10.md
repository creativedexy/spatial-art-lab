# Round 10: big animals, side on, more than a headshot
Clean portrait plates. The attached references are for LIGHT AND DARKNESS ONLY: one low light source, deep black beyond, photographic wildlife realism, particles catching the light. No ferns, no moss. Each plate its own environment (given below).

Framing, the key change from last round:
- **Side on (profile or three-quarter from the side), never front on.** The animal looks across the frame, with open dark space in front of its face.
- **More than a headshot:** head, neck and shoulders down to the front of the chest or forelegs (for the shark, the front half of the body with the pectoral fin and gills).
- **Not full width:** the animal takes up about 55-65% of the frame width, the rest is its environment and the dark. Placed on the lower-middle of the portrait frame.
