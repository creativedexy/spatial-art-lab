# Alignment stills, round 2: tracking and time, in living nature

**For:** Codex, free subscription, built-in image_gen. £0. No reference images.

Dex chose round 1's `02-tracking-live` (orb-weaver, pose skeleton, joint IDs) and `05-time-trail` (spider walking on moss as successive copies, present moment framed, timestamps). Round 2 combines them and puts nature first.

Save four PNGs at 1600 × 900 in `experiments/003-spider-probe/alignment/out2/`, plus `notes.md` and `prompts.json` as before.

## Rules (all frames)
- Same palette and rules as round 1 (brief.md): black ground, gold and white points, the machine visible as hairlines, tiny sparse monospace text with short real words, at least 40% true black.
- **Nature is paramount:** the spider is inside a real, specific habitat rendered as point clouds too: wet moss, bark, fern fronds, fallen leaves, dew on silk. The habitat takes up a real part of the frame, lit low as if by a single torch.
- Every frame shows a pose skeleton on the legs (hairline segments joining joints, small square markers with IDs like J4) AND time (trails, ghost copies or timestamps).
- It reads as one still from a slow, continuous camera move.

## The four frames
1. `07-bark-trail.png`: a wolf spider running across a mossy log, leaving five fading point-cloud copies of itself along its path; the skeleton and joint IDs on the current one only; a thin frame and timecode on the present.
2. `08-dew-web-track.png`: an orb-weaver repairing its web at dawn, dew drops as bright points along the threads; its legs tracked with a skeleton; faint trails show each leg's path over the last second.
3. `09-leaf-litter.png`: low macro view at ground level, a spider stepping between fallen leaves and fern fronds (all point clouds); a line of small timestamps follows its route; a bracket locks on to it.
4. `10-night-torch.png`: wider view, a patch of forest floor at night with three spiders tracked at once (IDs 01 to 03), each with a short motion trail, the habitat filling two thirds of the frame.
