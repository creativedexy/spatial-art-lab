# Round 8b: more creatures, same world

All six clean plates were generated natively at 1024 × 1536 with the built-in image generation tool, using V2, V3, and V4 from Round 7 as style references. Each project copy was then scaled to 1920 px tall and centre-cropped to 1080 × 1920 with `sips`.

## c1 — shark

- Result: A reef shark glides diagonally down through a kelp forest, surrounded by low warm torchlight and suspended particles against deep black water.
- Crop check: The complete shark remains inside the central 9:16 crop, with kelp habitat on all sides.

## c2 — octopus

- Result: An octopus spreads naturally over barnacled rock and wet weed, with curling arms and its eye catching the low torch.
- Crop check: The mantle, eye, and arm envelope remain within the central crop; the dense rock-pool habitat stays legible.

## c3 — seahorse

- Result: A seahorse stands upright in a seagrass meadow, its curled tail visibly gripping a blade while plankton sparks catch the light.
- Crop check: The whole animal and its seagrass hold remain centered and safe.

## c4 — owl

- Result: A tawny owl faces the camera from a wet moss-and-ivy branch, with restrained eye catchlights and forest darkness behind.
- Crop check: The entire owl, feet, and branch contact remain inside the central crop.

## c5 — panther

- Result: A melanistic leopard moves through wet ferns and leaf litter, its subtle rosettes, whiskers, and eyes revealed by grazing torchlight.
- Crop check: Head, torso, and visible limbs remain safe in the central 9:16 frame.

## c6 — wolf

- Result: A grey wolf stands beside a mossy fallen log amid wet ferns, head turned toward the light and breath faintly visible.
- Crop check: The complete standing body and all four paws remain inside the central crop.

## Shared direction

- Photographic macro or close wildlife realism.
- One low torch or moonlike source, warm grazing highlights, and deep black falloff.
- Rich real habitat with wet surfaces, dew, or suspended particles catching the light.
- Clean plates only: no HUD, text, lines, borders, or overlays.
