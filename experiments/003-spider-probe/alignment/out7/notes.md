# Round 7 portrait plates

All six plates were generated natively at 1024 × 1536 with the built-in image generation tool, then scaled to 1920 px tall and centre-cropped to 1080 × 1920 with `sips`. Coordinates below are rough subject centres in the final 9:16 plates, measured as `(x%, y%)` from the top-left. For the long-tentacled compass jellyfish, the coordinate tracks the bell/primary mass rather than the full tentacle envelope.
